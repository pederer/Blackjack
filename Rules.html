Blackjack Rules

This table follows the single-player rules shown here: blackjack pays 3:2, the dealer stands on soft 17, one split is allowed, Double Down is limited to opening hard totals of 9, 10, or 11, and insurance and surrender are not offered.

Goal and card values

Beat the dealer by finishing closer to 21 without going over. Number cards count as their number; jacks, queens, and kings count as 10. An ace counts as 1 or 11, whichever gives the hand the best total at or below 21.

How a round works

  • You and the dealer each receive two cards. One dealer card is hidden while you play.
  • Choose Hit to take a card or Stand to keep your total.
  • If your total exceeds 21, you bust and lose the hand.
  • After you finish, the dealer reveals the hidden card and draws on totals below 17. The dealer stands on all 17s, including soft 17.
  • If the dealer busts, every player hand that has not busted wins.
  • If neither hand busts, the higher total wins. An ordinary win pays 1:1; equal totals push and return the wager.

Blackjack and payouts

  • A blackjack is an ace and a 10-value card in the first two cards. It pays 3:2 when the dealer does not also have blackjack.
  • If both you and the dealer have blackjack, the hand pushes.
  • A dealer blackjack beats a player hand of 21 made with more than two cards.

Double and split

  • You may Double Down before hitting or splitting when your opening two-card hand has a hard total of 9, 10, or 11 (count an ace as 1 for this check). The wager is doubled, you receive one card, and your hand then stands. Double Down is not available after a split.
  • You may split one pair of cards with the same rank into two hands. No second split is allowed. Play each hand with Hit or Stand and settle it against the dealer separately. A 21 after splitting is paid as an ordinary win, not as blackjack.
  • After splitting aces, each hand receives one additional card and then stands.

Wagers

Wagers are whole-dollar amounts and cannot exceed the available bankroll. The opening bet is taken when the cards are dealt. Splitting and doubling require enough bankroll for the additional wager.

Not offered

This game does not offer insurance or surrender.
